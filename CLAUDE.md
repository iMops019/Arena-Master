# Arena Master

A game built on C-Engine. The engine is the git submodule in `engine/` (repo: iMops019/C-Engine, a local working copy lives at `C:\Users\conov\Documents\C-Engine`).

## Rules

- **Never edit files under `engine/`.** Engine changes go in the engine repo: commit and push there, then run `./update-engine.ps1` here to move the submodule pointer and commit the bump. Anything game-specific stays out of the engine; the engine's `Instructions.md` explains the split.
- The game talks to the engine only through `IGameContent` (`engine/src/CEngine.Core/IGameContent.cs`) and the public `EngineWindow` API. `ArenaMasterContent` in `src/ArenaMaster.Game` is the implementation.
- Game assets go under this repo's `assets/` (`models/`, `textures/`, `sounds/`). The Editor's Save Terrain / Save Scene write `assets/terrain.dat` and `assets/scene.json` here. Raw Meshy exports go in the git-ignored `assets/raw/`; convert them with `engine/tools/asset-pipeline` and commit only the `.glb`.
- Engine architecture and conventions: `engine/CLAUDE.md` (large; search it rather than reading it all) and `engine/Instructions.md`.

## Status

The game is a 3D fantasy survivor-like (Megabonk-style), single player. **Read `DESIGN.md` first**: it has the design, the ground rules (nothing from ConnEngine-Game; one class at a time, starting with the Ranger; classes share nothing), the build order and the open questions. Build-order steps 1 to 9 are all in. The user is happy with steps 1-7 (movement, shooting, levelling, the full run, items, camp and the Sharpshooter tree, swarms). Step 8 (meta progression) and the title screen's New Game button came next. Step 9: the second class, the **Paladin**, with its **Defiance** tree, and a weapon rack at camp to choose the class. The user likes it. Step 10: the third class, the **Mage**, with its **Frost** tree. Step 11: the fourth class, the **Shaman**, with its **Lightning Alignment** tree (Claude's design). Step 13 (after the Delve): the fifth class, the **Warrior**, with its **Berserker** tree (Berserking and rage are the user's; not yet played). Step 14: the sixth class, the **Priest**, with its **Unholy** tree (the skull, Plague, Death and Decay and Pestilence are the user's; not yet played). Step 12, the newest and not yet played: **the Delve** (a chart of floors and nodes, 10-minute Delve runs ending in a boss's cache, depth scaling and floor looks), **Boss nodes** (the Hollow King Unbound in an arena), **Delve Marks**, and **gear** (12 uniques in 3 slots). The classic 30-minute run is kept. On 2026-09-26 a balance pass made items rare and capped, gear 5%, silver about half, bounties harder (see DESIGN.md); the same day two fodder enemies were added, the **Ghoul Beast Rider** (charges) and the **Ghoul Tactician** (lobs bouncing bombs); the user played them and likes both. Next, per DESIGN.md: play and tune the classes and the Delve; what Delve Marks buy is the user's to design, and what duplicate gear is for.
- **Classes** go through one seam, `Classes/IHeroClass` (stats the content needs, movement, attacks, answering enemy blows, the level-up pool). Every hero's body is a skinned model drawn through `Classes/HeroBody` (the one piece of body code the classes share, like `PlayerHealth`: how a body stands on the ground, not how it fights). Its pure `HeroMotion` mixes the moving clips every hero model has (`Idle`, `Air`, and `Run` with its versions 45 and 90 degrees to each side), each run clip as many seconds long as its stride is metres, so the clip moves on by the distance the feet cover and a planted foot stays put. The two runs either side of the way it's going are mixed, backward plays them backward, and standing, running, turning round and the air fade rather than snap. Each class lays its own attack clip over the `chest` through the engine's `SetSkinnedPropBlend`: the Ranger's `Shoot` keeps time with the bow's cooldown (`RangerBow.DrawProgress`: nock, draw to the jaw, loose). The Paladin's `Smite`, the Mage's `Cast`, the Shaman's `Hurl`, the Warrior's `Cleave` and the Priest's `Hex` are one second each, with the blow landing at 0.5, and each class's `AttackTime` maps its own clock (`NovaIn`, `BarrageIn`, `CastIn`, `SwingIn`, the Priest's `CastIn`) onto that. Not yet seen in a running game window. `ArenaMasterContent` holds one of each (`RangerClass`, `PaladinClass`, `MageClass`, `ShamanClass`, `WarriorClass`, `PriestClass`) and runs `_hero`, the profile's `ActiveClass`; `UseClass` switches it and its tree. Classes share nothing else: never reuse Ranger code for the Paladin or the other way round.
- The game starts at **camp** (`Camp/Camp.cs`, `ArenaMasterContent.Camp.cs`): stations opened with E (item chest, passive tree, weapon rack with the class screen, bounty board, quartermaster, departure gate with the loadout screen). A run (`ArenaMasterContent.Run.cs`) starts from the gate, teleports to the middle of the map, and ends in a summary screen, then back to camp. Camp is walled in (`CampWalls`: an octagon of wall pieces and corner posts, the gate a gatehouse in its north side) and dressed (`CampLayout.Decor`, placed with `PlaceProp` in `BuildCamp` on the first frame of play, since `CreateProps` is one per file; the stations and fire pit stay in `CreateProps`), with low hills round it (`ShapeGround`). The quartermaster (`QuartermasterNpc`) is a skinned model posed each camp frame with its looping `Idle` clip, and taken out on a run. Travel between camp and a run goes through `Ui/ScreenFade` (`Travel`: the world pauses, fades to black, the move is made in the dark, fades back in). `ArenaMasterContent` is split into partial files: world and mode switching, `.Camp`, `.Run`, `.Hud`.
- `Delve/`: `DelveMap` (the floors and nodes, seeded by depth; `DelveNode`; `DelveSave` in the profile; `DelveRules`: open and cleared nodes, clearing opens the floor below, depth scaling, rewards, the arena's level and boss health), `DelveDirector` (the 10-minute schedule, pure), `DelveBands` (each band of floors' time of day, mist, weather and wisps), `BossArena` (the arena's layout, like `CampLayout`), and `RunPlan` (classic, Delve or Arena, and the node). `ArenaMasterContent.Delve.cs` runs them: `BeginDelve`, `UpdateDelve` (the director or the arena, the boss's stages), the Delve cache (`DropCache`, `UpdateCache`: touching it ends the run as `RunEnding.DelveCleared`), `SettleDelve` (the cache's payout, clearing the node), and `ApplyLook`/`RestoreCampLook`. A boss with `EnemyKind.Phases` changes its attacks, cooldown and speed as its health falls (`EnemyField.TakePhaseChanges`); an `AttackSpec.Chain` repeats an attack at once with half the wind-up; `AttackType.Barrage` drops fireballs from the sky on marked spots. The Hollow King Unbound is `DelveBosses.HollowKingUnbound`.
- `Gear/`: `GearCatalog` (18 uniques, 6 per slot: body armour, weapon, trinket), `GearSave` in the profile (owned, worn), `Grant` (a find: unowned, preferring an empty slot). Gear is a chance, not a promise: a cache rolls `DelveRules.RollsGear` (30% for an Armoury node, which only turns up from depth 6, `DelveMap.ArmouryFrom`, and 40% for a Boss node), and `GearCatalog.Grant` gives any piece, owned or not (`GearSave.Copies` counts duplicates; they do nothing yet). A floor has 5 nodes: 2-3 King nodes and the rest Descents (`DelveNodeKind.Descent`, `DelveNode.HasKing`: no King, the `DelveDirector` says `CacheDue` at 10:00 and the cache drops by the player). A cleared node is locked for good. Worn gear joins a run's bonuses through `ItemInventory.AddBonus` (not an item). The gear effects that act on their own are in `ItemEffects`: the returning gear shield (`PlayerHealth.Shield`, taken before `Barrier`) and the fire nova.
- `Progression/`: `Profile` + `ProfileStore` (the save, in `%AppData%/ArenaMaster/profile.json`); `MetaProgress` (silver from `RunRewards`, the Quartermaster's `Shop` upgrades, the `Elixirs` (Battle Elixirs: bought for the next run only, one of each, not by a class with its tree at level 6 or more; `Profile.Elixirs` holds the bought ones and `BeginRun` drinks them into the run's bonuses through `ItemInventory.AddBonus`; the Life Elixir is `ItemBonuses.MaxHealthMultiplier`, which every class's `MaxHealth` multiplies by), the `Bounties` that unlock the locked epics and legendaries, lifetime totals); and `PassiveTree` (`TreeProgress`: levels from experience, taking and refunding ranks). The Sharpshooter's nodes and what they add up to are in `Ranger/SharpshooterTree.cs`. Every node is playable. A node can be marked `Playable: false` to show as "coming soon" while its effect is being built. The per-hit majors (Fork, Storm of Splinters, One Shot One Kill) live in `RangerArrows` through `HitRules`; the timed ones (Sniper's Focus, Endless Quiver, Rain of Arrows) in `RangerBow`, with their clocks in the pure `BowCadence`.
- `Ui/`: `UiTheme` (the shared look and the `GameScreen` base), `ItemScreens` (item chest, loadout, and the `Loadout` rules: 5 items plus bought slots), `MetaScreens` (bounty board, quartermaster), `ClassScreen`, `PassiveTreeScreen` (draws any `TreeDefinition`: its lanes and flat stats are on the definition), `RunSummaryScreen` (with a Delve node's outcome), `DelveChartScreen`, `GearScreen` (the unique orange look is `UiTheme.Unique`), `ScreenFade`, `LevelUpScreen` (class-neutral `LevelUpCard`s, with rerolls and banishes; the class keeps its own pool and banished set).
- `ArenaMasterContent` builds a 512 m test map with hills, trees and rocks, turns on the engine's `ThirdPerson` view in Play mode, and runs everything below each frame. It also runs the level-up flow: kills drop gems, XP levels the player up, and each level sets the engine's `GamePaused` and shows `Ui/LevelUpScreen` (ImGui, drawn from `DrawOverlay`) until a pick is made. It draws the HUD: XP bar and level across the top, run clock, kills, HP, dash bar, damage numbers, a boss health bar, announcements, and the run summary. Dev keys F5/F6/F7/F9 in a run (skip a minute, spawn a brute, spawn the king, start a Monster Rush, in `DevKeys`) and F8 at camp (+1 tree level, in `UpdateCamp`) must go before a release. The Play host sets `BuiltInPanels = false` and `MapsMenu = false`, and adds a title-screen **New Game** button (`RequestNewGame`): behind a `ConfirmScreen`, it backs the save up (`ProfileStore.Backup`, never deletes) and starts a fresh `Profile`.
- `Ranger/`: `RangerClass` (the `IHeroClass`; Momentum and Headhunter live here), `RangerStats` (base numbers plus upgrades, items and tree; everything reads from it), `RangerUpgrades` (the level-up pool and rolling 3 choices), `RangerController` (run, Shift dash, body faces the aim), `RangerBow` (auto-fires at whatever the crosshair is on, fanning split shots), and `RangerArrows` (straight flight, pierce, chaining, crits, hit rules like elite damage and execute, sticking in the ground on a miss).
- `Mage/`: `MageClass` (the `IHeroClass`, the Frost Shield's clock and Ice Block; `Fight` and `AfterBlows` take no window, for the tests), `MageStats`, `MageUpgrades`, `MageController` (walk, blink), `FrostBarrage` (pure: the staggered homing bolts and their targeting, chill and freeze, Frost Blast, Splitting Ice, Comet, Blizzard), `FrostView` (bolts, bursts and shards as crowds), and `FrostTree` with `FrostBonuses`.
- `Shaman/`: `ShamanClass` (the `IHeroClass`; aims at the crosshair, answers blows with Static Skin and Lightning Reflexes; `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `ShamanStats`, `ShamanUpgrades`, `ShamanController` (walk, surge; tells the class when a surge begins), `RollingLightning` (pure: the lobbed, bouncing balls, zaps, forks, rods, and the majors; trees and rocks come in through an `ObstacleProbe`, which in the game is the engine's `EngineWindow.TouchesObstacle`), `StormView` (round pulsing balls in a crackling static shell with sparks, jagged arcs, bounce discharges and zaps, as crowds; the lob's arc height is `ShamanStats.LobHeight`, raised by the camera's pitch), and `AlignmentTree` with `AlignmentBonuses`.
- `Warrior/`: `WarriorClass` (the `IHeroClass`; the Cleave goes where the camera faces; rage, life leech, Blood Frenzy, and answering blows with Riposte, Fuelled by Pain, Blade Wall and Last Stand; `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `WarriorStats` (its `Rage` and `Frenzy` set by the class each frame), `WarriorUpgrades`, `WarriorController` (walk, battle charge), `CleaveAxes` (pure: the swings, each `CleaveWave` a wedge rolling out that hits what its front passes once, Echoing Cleave, Twin Fury, Whirlwind, Execute; and `Fury`, the rage), `WarriorView` (each wave front as a band of `cleave_wave.glb` pieces along its arc, any size), and `BerserkerTree` with `BerserkerBonuses`.
- `Priest/`: `PriestClass` (the `IHeroClass`; answers blocks with Death and Decay and Bone Armour, heals with Soul Harvest; `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `PriestStats`, `PriestUpgrades`, `PriestController` (walk, Rotting Step; says where the Priest vanished, for the rot), `PlagueSkulls` (pure: the seeking, piercing skulls, stacking `Infection`s of Plague, `RotPatch`es on the ground (circles and wedges), Death and Decay's `RotSpray`, Pestilence's `PlagueLeap`s, the Aura), `PriestView` (the skull, wisps, pools and its own mote particles, as crowds), and `UnholyTree` with `UnholyBonuses`.
- `Paladin/`: `PaladinClass` (the `IHeroClass`, and what answers enemy blows: shield bash, block heal, Holy Bastion, Retribution, Shield of Faith, Unbroken Vow; its `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `PaladinStats`, `PaladinUpgrades`, `PaladinController` (walk, shield rush), `HolyLight` (pure: the Holy Nova, echoes, holy circles, thorns, and the majors that add bursts), `HolyView` (novas and circles as crowds), and `DefianceTree` with `DefianceBonuses`.
- `Combat/`: general, not tied to a class. `EnemyField` is a pure simulation: spawns in a ring 24-38 m from the player (as many as the `RunDirector` asks for, 16 growing to 300), chases, keeps enemies apart, contact damage, hits and deaths. `EnemyGrid` files enemies by 4 m cell for spacing and arrow hit lookups. `EnemyView` draws each kind of enemy as one engine animated crowd (`SetSkinnedCrowd`, instanced, each copy at its own clip and time) with a flinch and flash and a death sink; `EnemyMotion` (pure) picks each enemy's clip: `Idle`, `Walk` (a stride as long in seconds as in metres, moved on by the ground the enemy covers, so its feet stay planted), the clip named after its current `AttackType` with the wind-up, blow and recovery laid over 0-0.4, 0.4-0.7 and 0.7-1 of it, frozen in place while frozen, and `Die` over the `DeathDuration`; a change of clip fades. A crate (`IsProp`) is still a plain crowd. Arrows and XP gems are crowds too. Placed props are only for the few: chests and orbs. Kinds live in `EnemyKind` (Ghoul, Crossbow Ghoul, Ghoul Mage, Ghoul Beast Rider and Ghoul Tactician fodder, Ghoul Brute elite, Hollow King boss), each with telegraphed `AttackSpec`s (wind-up, active, recover). An `EnemyKind.Behaviour` of `Stalk` (the Ghoul Beast) replaces chasing with `EnemyField.Stalk`: circling toward the player's back (`PlayerTarget.Facing`, the camera's flat facing), bolting when looked at or hurt (`Enemy.Mood`), pouncing (a `Lunge`) only with an opening (back turned or stunned), fleeing after; the HUD's `DrawPounceWarnings` marks a pounce winding up. The Beast Rider's charge is a `Lunge` whose lane follows the player for its `Tracking` seconds of the wind-up, then locks. The Tactician's `AttackType.Lob` throws an `EnemyBomb` (`EnemyField.Bombs`): it lands short, bounces `Count` times toward the player, rolls to a stop and goes off, or goes off at once on the player, an obstacle (`EnemyField.Obstacles`, the engine's `TouchesObstacle`, set in `BeginRun`) or a steep slope; the view draws a green ring under it. The ranged kinds' `Shoot` looses an `EnemyBolt` (the field moves them and strikes the player): a bolt has to hit, a shot with a `Splash` (the fireball) is aimed at the ground under the player and bursts (`Blasts`), and the view marks its landing spot. `StandOff` stops a ranged kind short, and its `HeldModel` (crossbow, flame) is drawn as its own animated crowd on the same skeleton and clip, which `EnemyView.Glow` brightens through the wind-up. The director mixes them into the fodder through the field's `Mix` (kind and share each; `RunDirector.MixAt`, which the Delve's director uses too). `EnemyField` runs the attacks, rolls the player's block chance (`PlayerTarget.BlockChance`; a block stops damage, shove and stun), lists each frame's blows that reached the player in `Strikes`, and finds enemies in an area with `Within`. `PlayerHealth` has `Deflect` (a block's grace, no damage), `LastStands` and `Barrier` (taken before health). An `Enemy` can be chilled (`Chill`, slows `WalkSpeed`) or frozen (`Freeze`: `Move` skips it). `EnemyView` also draws the ground telegraphs (lane, ring and filling disc, shockwave ring), as crowds. `RunDirector` is the 30-minute schedule. `MonsterRush` is a random event on top of either director (every few minutes, 30 s of a much bigger crowd topped up at once; `Apply` after the director each frame). `PlayerCondition` holds knock-back and stun. `Armour` (the percentage cut, armour / (armour + 200); a class's Defense of 8 a tree level; body armour gear adds `ItemBonuses.Armour`): the content folds it into `PlayerHealth.DamageTaken` (`ArmourNow`) and the HUD shows it. `EnemyField.FodderAttackCap` holds how many fodder may be at each kind of attack at once (shots 12, bombs 6, charges 8; elites and bosses never held). `MonsterRarity` (Normal, Magic, Rare, Legendary): `RarityTraits` (health, damage, attack speed, walking speed, experience, chest chance, size, ring model) and `RarityOdds`, which the directors set on the field's `RarityOdds`; `EnemyField.Spawn` rolls it for everything but bosses and props (tests get none unless they set it). An `Enemy` carries its `Rarity`, `Name`, `Experience` and `AttackSpeed` (its attack phases and cooldowns run that much quicker); `TakeLegendarySpawns` feeds an announcement, `LootField.RollRarityDrop` the chest, `EnemyView` the ring and size, the HUD the name tags. Also here: `Experience` (level curve 5n), `XpGems` (gems, their magnet pull, and their view), `PlayerHealth` and `DamageNumbers`.
- `Items/`: shared loot, not tied to a class. `Items.cs` has the item catalog (named `RunItem`, because the engine already has an `ItemDefinition`), rarity odds, `ItemBonuses` (bonuses add, multipliers multiply), and `ItemInventory`. Items persist in the profile's stash. A run's bonuses come only from `RunItems.Carried`: the loadout's items (every copy owned when it set out), fixed for the run. Finds go to the chest and `RunItems.Found`, and never boost the run they were found in (the user's rule). `Loot.cs` has `LootField` (chests on a timer, elite/boss chests, rare fodder drops, pickup on touch) and `LootView`. There are 64 items; every epic and legendary is locked behind one bounty. Items are rare (about one a classic run: `LootField`'s drop chances for world chests, elites, bosses and fodder) and **capped**: `RunItem.MaxStack` (commons 3, rares 2, the rest 1) is how many copies count in `ItemInventory.Bonuses`; `Bounties.CanDrop` keeps locked and maxed items out of drops. A class reads the bonuses that change its own attacks through its stats (`RangerStats.Items`, `PaladinStats.Items`, `MageStats.Items`, `ShamanStats.Items`; chains, area multiplier and projectiles each mean something per class; attack speed through `AttackSpeedNow`, which carries Berserker's Band). What changes every hit whoever lands it (chill, freeze, damage to chilled, frozen and elite enemies) is the enemy field's `HitEffects`, set at the start of a run (`ItemEffects.HitEffectsOf`). `ItemEffects.cs` runs the items that act on their own: thorns, the ward, Bloodstone, Martyr's Crown, the Aegis burst, the Thunderstone, the Phoenix Feather (a class answers its own last stand first; the rest are the feather's). The content handles regeneration, heal-on-kill, the XP and silver bonuses, rerolls and damage taken. The item chest, loadout and bounty board scroll.
- `World/`: `Crates.cs` has `CrateField` (breakable crates turning up around the player, standing on the enemy field as `EnemyKind.Crate`, a prop: `IsProp` makes it stand still, never strike, not count as a kill or toward the swarm; the random pickup a broken one leaves; taking pickups) and `CrateView`. `ArenaMasterContent.Crates.cs` wires it into the run and says what each pickup does (magnet via `XpGemField.AttractAll`, food, silver added at the run's end, bomb, frenzy via `ItemBonuses.Frenzy`, big gem).
- The heroes (six) are skinned stand-ins made by `tools/hero_models.py` (run by `make_placeholder_models.py`, written to `<class>_hero.glb`) on the shared skeleton and maths in `tools/hero_rig.py`. That covers the joints (the `spine` undoes the hips' turn so the chest faces the aim; props like a bowstring's `nock`, a flail, a cape, robe and tabard flaps), leg IK, and the run cycle posed from where the feet go (planted feet slide back exactly as far as the body goes forward, heel to toe). Shields and weapons are held out from the body by aiming the arms (`arm_ik`) and turning the hand (`face`). `python tools/preview_hero.py <hero> out.png --view=side|front|three|back --clips=Run,Shoot` draws contact sheets with Pillow, to look at poses without the game. `HeroModelTests` checks every hero's clips, feet and attacks.
- The enemies are skinned the same way, in `tools/enemy_models.py` on `hero_rig.py`: `ghoul.glb`, `crossbow_ghoul.glb` with `ghoul_crossbow.glb`, `ghoul_mage.glb` with `ghoul_flame.glb` (the flame on its own joint, growing through a cast), `ghoul_beast.glb` (the rider's beast alone, on `BEAST_JOINTS`), `beast_rider.glb` (a four-legged beast on its own `Skeleton`: the hind legs are the usual `thigh`/`shin`/`foot` joints, the forelegs `fore_*`, the rider hangs from the `spine`), `ghoul_tactician.glb` with `ghoul_tactician_bomb.glb` (the bomb on its own joint, gone once thrown), `brute.glb`, `hollow_king.glb` and `hollow_king_unbound.glb` (the king at 1.32 times). Each has `Idle`, `Walk`, `Die` and a one-second clip per attack type it has. Walking strides are sized so the legs reach the ground (`EnemyModelTests` holds a foot on the ground through every walk). `preview_hero.py` draws them too (`ghoul`, `brute`, ...).
- Other models are stand-ins generated by `tools/make_placeholder_models.py` (the cleave wave piece, arrow, the Tactician's bomb with its ring and burst, the Priest's skull, wisps, motes, ash and rot pool, the crate and its pickups, holy nova ring, holy circle, frost bolt, frost blast ring, frost shard, the Aegis burst ring, ghoul, brute, hollow king, xp gem, chest, loot beam, item orbs, the flat telegraph markers, and the camp: tent, fire pit, stash, target, gate, board, stall, weapon rack, the palisade, and its dressing). Only the quartermaster is animated: `write_skinned_glb` writes a skinned model (each face on one joint) with clips sampled from a pose function; the engine's `SkinnedModel` loads it (`QuartermasterTests` checks it does). Adding a colour to its palette changes every model's texture, so rerun it and commit them all.
- Tests live in `tests/ArenaMaster.Game.Tests` (run `dotnet test ArenaMaster.slnx`). Keep simulation logic free of `EngineWindow` so it stays testable.
