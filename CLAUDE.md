# Arena Master

A game built on C-Engine. The engine is the git submodule in `engine/` (repo: iMops019/C-Engine, a local working copy lives at `C:\Users\conov\Documents\C-Engine`).

## Rules

- **Never edit files under `engine/`.** Engine changes go in the engine repo: commit and push there, then run `./update-engine.ps1` here to move the submodule pointer and commit the bump. Anything game-specific stays out of the engine; the engine's `Instructions.md` explains the split.
- The game talks to the engine only through `IGameContent` (`engine/src/CEngine.Core/IGameContent.cs`) and the public `EngineWindow` API. `ArenaMasterContent` in `src/ArenaMaster.Game` is the implementation.
- Game assets go under this repo's `assets/` (`models/`, `textures/`, `sounds/`). The Editor's Save Terrain / Save Scene write `assets/terrain.dat` and `assets/scene.json` here. Raw Meshy exports go in the git-ignored `assets/raw/`; convert them with `engine/tools/asset-pipeline` and commit only the `.glb`.
- Engine architecture and conventions: `engine/CLAUDE.md` (large; search it rather than reading it all) and `engine/Instructions.md`.

## Status

The game is a 3D fantasy survivor-like (Megabonk-style), single player. **Read `DESIGN.md` first**: it has the design, the ground rules (nothing from ConnEngine-Game; one class at a time, starting with the Ranger; classes share nothing), the build order and the open questions. Build-order steps 1 to 6 are in. The user is happy with steps 1-5. Step 6 (camp, save file, persistent items and loadouts, the Sharpshooter passive tree) is waiting for them to try it.
- The game starts at **camp** (`Camp/Camp.cs`, `ArenaMasterContent.Camp.cs`): stations opened with E (item chest, passive tree, departure gate with the loadout screen). A run (`ArenaMasterContent.Run.cs`) starts from the gate, teleports to the middle of the map, and ends in a summary screen, then back to camp. `ArenaMasterContent` is split into partial files: world and mode switching, `.Camp`, `.Run`, `.Hud`.
- `Progression/`: `Profile` + `ProfileStore` (the save, in `%AppData%/ArenaMaster/profile.json`) and `PassiveTree` (`TreeProgress`: levels from experience, taking and refunding ranks). The Sharpshooter's nodes and what they add up to are in `Ranger/SharpshooterTree.cs`. Nodes whose effect isn't built yet are `Playable: false` and show as "coming soon".
- `Ui/`: `UiTheme` (the shared look and the `GameScreen` base), `ItemScreens` (item chest, loadout, and the 5-item `Loadout` rules), `PassiveTreeScreen`, `RunSummaryScreen`, `LevelUpScreen`.
- `ArenaMasterContent` builds a 512 m test map with hills, trees and rocks, turns on the engine's `ThirdPerson` view in Play mode, and runs everything below each frame. It also runs the level-up flow: kills drop gems, XP levels the player up, and each level sets the engine's `GamePaused` and shows `Ui/LevelUpScreen` (ImGui, drawn from `DrawOverlay`) until a pick is made. It draws the HUD: XP bar and level across the top, run clock, kills, HP, dash bar, damage numbers, a boss health bar, announcements, and the run summary. Dev keys F5/F6/F7 (skip a minute, spawn a brute, spawn the king) are in `DevKeys` and must go before a release. The Play host sets `BuiltInPanels = false`.
- `Ranger/`: `RangerStats` (base numbers plus upgrades, items and tree; everything reads from it), `RangerUpgrades` (the level-up pool and rolling 3 choices), `RangerController` (run, Shift dash, body faces the aim), `RangerBow` (auto-fires at whatever the crosshair is on, fanning split shots), and `RangerArrows` (straight flight, pierce, chaining, crits, hit rules like elite damage and execute, sticking in the ground on a miss).
- `Combat/`: general, not tied to a class. `EnemyField` is a pure simulation: spawns in a ring 24-38 m from the player (kept at 14 alive), chases, keeps enemies apart, contact damage, hits and deaths. `EnemyView` draws each enemy as a placed prop with a bob, a flinch and a death sink. Kinds live in `EnemyKind` (Ghoul fodder, Ghoul Brute elite, Hollow King boss), each with telegraphed `AttackSpec`s (wind-up, active, recover). `EnemyField` runs the attacks. `EnemyView` also draws the ground telegraphs (lane, ring and filling disc, shockwave ring). `RunDirector` is the 30-minute schedule. `PlayerCondition` holds knock-back and stun. Also here: `Experience` (level curve 5n), `XpGems` (gems, their magnet pull, and their view), `PlayerHealth` and `DamageNumbers`.
- `Items/`: shared loot, not tied to a class. `Items.cs` has the item catalog (named `RunItem`, because the engine already has an `ItemDefinition`), rarity odds, `ItemBonuses` (bonuses add, multipliers multiply), and `ItemInventory`. Items persist in the profile's stash; a run starts with the loadout's items (every copy) and adds what it finds. `Loot.cs` has `LootField` (chests on a timer, elite/boss chests, rare fodder drops, pickup on touch) and `LootView`. A class reads the bonuses through its stats (`RangerStats.Items`). The content handles regeneration, heal-on-kill, the XP bonus and damage taken.
- Models are stand-ins generated by `tools/make_placeholder_models.py` (ranger, arrow, ghoul, brute, hollow king, xp gem, chest, loot beam, item orbs, the flat telegraph markers, and the camp: tent, fire pit, stash, target, gate). None are animated.
- Tests live in `tests/ArenaMaster.Game.Tests` (run `dotnet test ArenaMaster.slnx`). Keep simulation logic free of `EngineWindow` so it stays testable.

Don't open a visible game window without asking. Play mode grabs the mouse, and the user may be using the PC.
