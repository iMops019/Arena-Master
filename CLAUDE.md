# Arena Master

A game built on C-Engine. The engine is the git submodule in `engine/` (repo: iMops019/C-Engine, a local working copy lives at `C:\Users\conov\Documents\C-Engine`).

## Rules

- **Never edit files under `engine/`.** Engine changes go in the engine repo: commit and push there, then run `./update-engine.ps1` here to move the submodule pointer and commit the bump. Anything game-specific stays out of the engine; the engine's `Instructions.md` explains the split.
- The game talks to the engine only through `IGameContent` (`engine/src/CEngine.Core/IGameContent.cs`) and the public `EngineWindow` API. `ArenaMasterContent` in `src/ArenaMaster.Game` is the implementation.
- Game assets go under this repo's `assets/` (`models/`, `textures/`, `sounds/`). The Editor's Save Terrain / Save Scene write `assets/terrain.dat` and `assets/scene.json` here. Raw Meshy exports go in the git-ignored `assets/raw/`; convert them with `engine/tools/asset-pipeline` and commit only the `.glb`.
- Engine architecture and conventions: `engine/CLAUDE.md` (large; search it rather than reading it all) and `engine/Instructions.md`.

## Status

The game is a 3D fantasy survivor-like (Megabonk-style), single player. **Read `DESIGN.md` first**: it has the design, the ground rules (nothing from ConnEngine-Game; one class at a time, starting with the Ranger; classes share nothing), the build order and the open questions. Build-order steps 1 to 9 are all in. The user is happy with steps 1-7 (movement, shooting, levelling, the full run, items, camp and the Sharpshooter tree, swarms). Step 8 (meta progression) and the title screen's New Game button came next. Step 9: the second class, the **Paladin**, with its **Defiance** tree, and a weapon rack at camp to choose the class. The user likes it. Step 10: the third class, the **Mage**, with its **Frost** tree. Step 11, the newest and not yet played: the fourth class, the **Shaman**, with its **Lightning Alignment** tree (Claude's design). Next, per DESIGN.md: play and tune the four classes.
- **Classes** go through one seam, `Classes/IHeroClass` (stats the content needs, movement, attacks, answering enemy blows, the level-up pool). `ArenaMasterContent` holds one of each (`RangerClass`, `PaladinClass`, `MageClass`, `ShamanClass`) and runs `_hero`, the profile's `ActiveClass`; `UseClass` switches it and its tree. Classes share nothing else: never reuse Ranger code for the Paladin or the other way round.
- The game starts at **camp** (`Camp/Camp.cs`, `ArenaMasterContent.Camp.cs`): stations opened with E (item chest, passive tree, weapon rack with the class screen, bounty board, quartermaster, departure gate with the loadout screen). A run (`ArenaMasterContent.Run.cs`) starts from the gate, teleports to the middle of the map, and ends in a summary screen, then back to camp. Camp is walled in (`CampWalls`: an octagon of wall pieces and corner posts, the gate a gatehouse in its north side) and dressed (`CampLayout.Decor`, placed with `PlaceProp` in `BuildCamp` on the first frame of play, since `CreateProps` is one per file; the stations and fire pit stay in `CreateProps`), with low hills round it (`ShapeGround`). The quartermaster (`QuartermasterNpc`) is a skinned model posed each camp frame with its looping `Idle` clip, and taken out on a run. Travel between camp and a run goes through `Ui/ScreenFade` (`Travel`: the world pauses, fades to black, the move is made in the dark, fades back in). `ArenaMasterContent` is split into partial files: world and mode switching, `.Camp`, `.Run`, `.Hud`.
- `Progression/`: `Profile` + `ProfileStore` (the save, in `%AppData%/ArenaMaster/profile.json`); `MetaProgress` (silver from `RunRewards`, the Quartermaster's `Shop` upgrades, the `Bounties` that unlock the locked epics and legendaries, lifetime totals); and `PassiveTree` (`TreeProgress`: levels from experience, taking and refunding ranks). The Sharpshooter's nodes and what they add up to are in `Ranger/SharpshooterTree.cs`. Every node is playable. A node can be marked `Playable: false` to show as "coming soon" while its effect is being built. The per-hit majors (Fork, Storm of Splinters, One Shot One Kill) live in `RangerArrows` through `HitRules`; the timed ones (Sniper's Focus, Endless Quiver, Rain of Arrows) in `RangerBow`, with their clocks in the pure `BowCadence`.
- `Ui/`: `UiTheme` (the shared look and the `GameScreen` base), `ItemScreens` (item chest, loadout, and the `Loadout` rules: 5 items plus bought slots), `MetaScreens` (bounty board, quartermaster), `ClassScreen`, `PassiveTreeScreen` (draws any `TreeDefinition`: its lanes and flat stats are on the definition), `RunSummaryScreen`, `LevelUpScreen` (class-neutral `LevelUpCard`s, with rerolls and banishes; the class keeps its own pool and banished set).
- `ArenaMasterContent` builds a 512 m test map with hills, trees and rocks, turns on the engine's `ThirdPerson` view in Play mode, and runs everything below each frame. It also runs the level-up flow: kills drop gems, XP levels the player up, and each level sets the engine's `GamePaused` and shows `Ui/LevelUpScreen` (ImGui, drawn from `DrawOverlay`) until a pick is made. It draws the HUD: XP bar and level across the top, run clock, kills, HP, dash bar, damage numbers, a boss health bar, announcements, and the run summary. Dev keys F5/F6/F7 in a run (skip a minute, spawn a brute, spawn the king, in `DevKeys`) and F8 at camp (+1 tree level, in `UpdateCamp`) must go before a release. The Play host sets `BuiltInPanels = false` and `MapsMenu = false`, and adds a title-screen **New Game** button (`RequestNewGame`): behind a `ConfirmScreen`, it backs the save up (`ProfileStore.Backup`, never deletes) and starts a fresh `Profile`.
- `Ranger/`: `RangerClass` (the `IHeroClass`; Momentum and Headhunter live here), `RangerStats` (base numbers plus upgrades, items and tree; everything reads from it), `RangerUpgrades` (the level-up pool and rolling 3 choices), `RangerController` (run, Shift dash, body faces the aim), `RangerBow` (auto-fires at whatever the crosshair is on, fanning split shots), and `RangerArrows` (straight flight, pierce, chaining, crits, hit rules like elite damage and execute, sticking in the ground on a miss).
- `Mage/`: `MageClass` (the `IHeroClass`, the Frost Shield's clock and Ice Block; `Fight` and `AfterBlows` take no window, for the tests), `MageStats`, `MageUpgrades`, `MageController` (walk, blink), `FrostBarrage` (pure: the staggered homing bolts and their targeting, chill and freeze, Frost Blast, Splitting Ice, Comet, Blizzard), `FrostView` (bolts, bursts and shards as crowds), and `FrostTree` with `FrostBonuses`.
- `Shaman/`: `ShamanClass` (the `IHeroClass`; aims at the crosshair, answers blows with Static Skin and Lightning Reflexes; `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `ShamanStats`, `ShamanUpgrades`, `ShamanController` (walk, surge; tells the class when a surge begins), `RollingLightning` (pure: the lobbed, bouncing balls, zaps, forks, rods, and the majors; trees and rocks come in through an `ObstacleProbe`, which in the game is the engine's `EngineWindow.TouchesObstacle`), `StormView` (balls, jagged arcs and zaps as crowds), and `AlignmentTree` with `AlignmentBonuses`.
- `Paladin/`: `PaladinClass` (the `IHeroClass`, and what answers enemy blows: shield bash, block heal, Holy Bastion, Retribution, Shield of Faith, Unbroken Vow; its `Fight` and `AnswerStrikes` take no window, for the tests), `PaladinStats`, `PaladinUpgrades`, `PaladinController` (walk, shield rush), `HolyLight` (pure: the Holy Nova, echoes, holy circles, thorns, and the majors that add bursts), `HolyView` (novas and circles as crowds), and `DefianceTree` with `DefianceBonuses`.
- `Combat/`: general, not tied to a class. `EnemyField` is a pure simulation: spawns in a ring 24-38 m from the player (as many as the `RunDirector` asks for, 16 growing to 300), chases, keeps enemies apart, contact damage, hits and deaths. `EnemyGrid` files enemies by 4 m cell for spacing and arrow hit lookups. `EnemyView` draws each kind of enemy as one engine crowd (`SetCrowd`, instanced) with a bob, a flinch and flash, and a death sink. Arrows and XP gems are crowds too. Placed props are only for the few: the Ranger, chests and orbs, and the ground telegraphs. Kinds live in `EnemyKind` (Ghoul, Crossbow Ghoul and Ghoul Mage fodder, Ghoul Brute elite, Hollow King boss), each with telegraphed `AttackSpec`s (wind-up, active, recover). The ranged kinds' `Shoot` looses an `EnemyBolt` (the field moves them and strikes the player): a bolt has to hit, a shot with a `Splash` (the fireball) is aimed at the ground under the player and bursts (`Blasts`), and the view marks its landing spot. `StandOff` stops a ranged kind short, and its `HeldModel` (crossbow, flame) is drawn as its own crowd that `EnemyView.Glow` brightens through the wind-up. The director mixes them into the fodder through the field's `Mix` (kind and share each). `EnemyField` runs the attacks, rolls the player's block chance (`PlayerTarget.BlockChance`; a block stops damage, shove and stun), lists each frame's blows that reached the player in `Strikes`, and finds enemies in an area with `Within`. `PlayerHealth` has `Deflect` (a block's grace, no damage), `LastStands` and `Barrier` (taken before health). An `Enemy` can be chilled (`Chill`, slows `WalkSpeed`) or frozen (`Freeze`: `Move` skips it). `EnemyView` also draws the ground telegraphs (lane, ring and filling disc, shockwave ring). `RunDirector` is the 30-minute schedule. `PlayerCondition` holds knock-back and stun. Also here: `Experience` (level curve 5n), `XpGems` (gems, their magnet pull, and their view), `PlayerHealth` and `DamageNumbers`.
- `Items/`: shared loot, not tied to a class. `Items.cs` has the item catalog (named `RunItem`, because the engine already has an `ItemDefinition`), rarity odds, `ItemBonuses` (bonuses add, multipliers multiply), and `ItemInventory`. Items persist in the profile's stash. A run's bonuses come only from `RunItems.Carried`: the loadout's items (every copy owned when it set out), fixed for the run. Finds go to the chest and `RunItems.Found`, and never boost the run they were found in (the user's rule). `Loot.cs` has `LootField` (chests on a timer, elite/boss chests, rare fodder drops, pickup on touch) and `LootView`. There are 46 items; every epic and legendary is locked behind one bounty. A class reads the bonuses that change its own attacks through its stats (`RangerStats.Items`, `PaladinStats.Items`, `MageStats.Items`, `ShamanStats.Items`; chains, area multiplier and projectiles each mean something per class; attack speed through `AttackSpeedNow`, which carries Berserker's Band). What changes every hit whoever lands it (chill, freeze, damage to chilled, frozen and elite enemies) is the enemy field's `HitEffects`, set at the start of a run (`ItemEffects.HitEffectsOf`). `ItemEffects.cs` runs the items that act on their own: thorns, the ward, Bloodstone, Martyr's Crown, the Aegis burst, the Thunderstone, the Phoenix Feather (a class answers its own last stand first; the rest are the feather's). The content handles regeneration, heal-on-kill, the XP and silver bonuses, rerolls and damage taken. The item chest, loadout and bounty board scroll.
- `World/`: `Crates.cs` has `CrateField` (breakable crates turning up around the player, standing on the enemy field as `EnemyKind.Crate`, a prop: `IsProp` makes it stand still, never strike, not count as a kill or toward the swarm; the random pickup a broken one leaves; taking pickups) and `CrateView`. `ArenaMasterContent.Crates.cs` wires it into the run and says what each pickup does (magnet via `XpGemField.AttractAll`, food, silver added at the run's end, bomb, frenzy via `ItemBonuses.Frenzy`, big gem).
- Models are stand-ins generated by `tools/make_placeholder_models.py` (ranger, paladin, mage, arrow, the crate and its pickups, holy nova ring, holy circle, frost bolt, frost blast ring, frost shard, the Aegis burst ring, ghoul, brute, hollow king, xp gem, chest, loot beam, item orbs, the flat telegraph markers, and the camp: tent, fire pit, stash, target, gate, board, stall, weapon rack, the palisade, and its dressing). Only the quartermaster is animated: `write_skinned_glb` writes a skinned model (each face on one joint) with clips sampled from a pose function; the engine's `SkinnedModel` loads it (`QuartermasterTests` checks it does). Adding a colour to its palette changes every model's texture, so rerun it and commit them all.
- Tests live in `tests/ArenaMaster.Game.Tests` (run `dotnet test ArenaMaster.slnx`). Keep simulation logic free of `EngineWindow` so it stays testable.

Don't open a visible game window without asking. Play mode grabs the mouse, and the user may be using the PC.
